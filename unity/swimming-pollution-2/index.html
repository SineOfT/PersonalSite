Swimming Pollution Game 2

This game discusses the issues of global pollution and littering, all while presented in a fun game! You are in the ocean collecting garbage, which, if it hits a fish, will cause them to become unable to swim. Try to survive as long as you can, and be careful! As more time passes, new fish appear as well new obstacles that make it harder to protect the wildlife!

I would love to complete this game and publish it someday!

Controls

  • Hold ⬅︎ to move left
  • Hold ⮕ to move right
  • Hold ⬆︎ to move up
  • Hold ⬇︎ to move down
