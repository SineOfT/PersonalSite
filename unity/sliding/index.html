Penguin Sliding Game

This game is inspired by curling, where the goal is to get as close to the target as you can. However, in this game, you cannot go into the target, as it is a hole. The 'stones' are penguins, where they can be launched, curled, and placed in order to block the other player from getting close to the target. The position of the target also moves, so strategize tactically!

I would love to complete this game and publish it someday!

Controls

  • Hold a point on the Screen inside the center circle to begin launching the closest penguin
  • Release in the direction you want to launch the penguin — the longer time you hold down, the faster they will be launched
  • Tap a point on the Screen to the left/right of the penguin while they are swimming to rotate their movement to the left/right, and create a curl
