3D Golf

The objective of this game is to putt the ball into the hole! It is quite an early prototype, where I tested how I could project three-dimensional space onto a two-dimensional screen. Have fun, and try to get a hole in one (I think it's possible)!

Controls

  • Hold a point on the Screen to putt ball
    • the x-position (left/right) controls how far left/right the ball is putt (center is 0)
    • the y-position (up/down) controls how far forward/backward the ball is putt (center is 0)
    • the time held controls how high the ball is putt
