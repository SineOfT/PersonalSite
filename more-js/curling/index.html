Curling

Just like Curling, the goal of this game is to get as many stones the closest to the button (center of ring) each round! However, unlike the sport, this game allows for more than two players to play at a time (up to eight)! It is surprisingly fun to play, so enjoy!

Controls

  • Before starting the game, you need to enter some settings:
    • When prompted Number of Players, type the number of players playing (from 1 to 8)
    • When prompted Stones per Round, type the number for the number of stones each player uses each round
    • When prompted Number of Rounds, type the number for the number of rounds you will play
    Click Submit to comfirm your options
    • When prompted Player _'s Stone Color, pick a color for the stones you want (2+ players can not pick the same color)
    Click Submit to comfirm your colors

Keyboard

  • Tap ⬆︎ to increase the curl of the stone counter-clockwise
  • Tap ⬇︎ to increase the curl of the stone clockwise
  • Hold a point on the Screen on the starting point of the ice to begin launching the stone
  • Release in the direction you want to launch the stone — the further away, the faster the stone will move
  • Move your Cursor to sweep
  • Tap ▼ / ▲ button to show/hide scoreboard

Touch Screen

  • Tap a point on the Screen on either side of the ice to change the curl of the stone (closer to the top makes a stronger counter-clockwise curl, vice versa)
  • Hold a point on the Screen on the starting point of the ice to begin launching the stone
  • Release in the direction you want to launch the stone — the further away, the faster the stone will move
  • Hold and move on the Screen to sweep
  • Tap ▼ / ▲ button to show/hide scoreboard
