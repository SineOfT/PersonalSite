Boat Physics Simulation

This is a visual simulation of how a boat may appear on rough waters. It may not be super physically accurate, however it can definitely work within a full scale game! The waves are simple sinusoidals, but in theory it should work with any shape wave. I would love to extend this program further and use it in a future game!

Controls

Keyboard

  • Hold ⬅︎ to move left
  • Hold ⮕ to move right

Touch Screen

  • ⬅︎
  • ⮕
  • Hold Left of Screen to move left
  • Hold Right of Screen to move right
