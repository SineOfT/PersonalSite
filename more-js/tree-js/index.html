JavaScript Tree Generator

This program generates five random shaped trees on every refresh. There are also four types of trees — trees with no leaves, trees with leaves, trees with flowers, and trees with both leaves and flowers. While they are pixelated, you can increase/decrease the resolution to see how they would look as pixel art or more realistic renders. Keep refreshing the browser, and see what interesting creations pop up!

Controls

  • Hold ⬅︎ to decrease the resolution of the trees
  • Hold ⮕ to increase the resolution of the trees
