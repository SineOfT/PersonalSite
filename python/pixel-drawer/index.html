Pixel Drawer

This program allows you to draw pixel art! Choose from any of the preset colors, and let your creative side take over!

Controls

  • When prompted What width canvas do you want?, type a number from 1 to 30
  • When prompted What height canvas do you want?, type a number from 1 to 30
  • Tap on a pixel to color it in
  • Tap ⬆︎ / ⬇︎ to scroll through the colors
