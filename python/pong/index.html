Pong

Just like classic Pong, the goal of this game is to not have the ball pass by your paddle! Continue trying to bounce the ball until the opponent makes a mistake!

Controls

  • When prompted How many players will play?, type either 0, 1, or 2 (The other players will be controled by computers)
  • Hold w (left player) or ⬆︎ (right player) to move up
  • Hold s (left player) or ⬇︎ (right player) to move down
