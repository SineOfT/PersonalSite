Last Card (UNO)

Just like UNO, the goal of this game is to place all of your cards before everyone else! You can only play a card if it is either the same color or suite than the card that is in play. You can also stack! Play among a group of people, or against computers!

Controls

  • When prompted How many players?, type the number of total players (controlled and computer) that are playing
  • When prompted How many players are being controlled?, type the number of players in the game being controlled
  • When prompted How fast do you want the test to move?, type either slow, medium, or fast — use slow if playing with multiple players, as it allows time to hide the screen
  • When prompted What card will you play?, type
    • the name of a playable card in your hand
    • pass to draw a card and pass your turn
    • last card to call last card
  • When prompted What color do you want to change to?, type either red, yellow, green, or blue
  • When prompted Play another card?, type either yes or no
