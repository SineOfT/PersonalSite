Tetris

Just like classic Tetris, the goal of this game is to get as many lines cleared as you can! Be careful however, as the more lines you clear, the faster the blocks begin to fall!

Controls

  • Tap ⬅︎ to move left
  • Tap ⮕ to move right
  • Tap ⬇︎ to move down
  • Tap ⬆︎ to hard drop
  • Tap z to rotate counter-clockwise
  • Tap x to rotate clockwise
