Racing Game

The goal of this game is to complete a lap in the shortest time, without ever exiting the track! As the car can get quite unwieldy at high speeds, it can become a fair challenge to improve your time. Can you go faster than my high score of 195?

Controls

  • Hold ⬅︎ to move left
  • Hold ⮕ to move right
  • Hold ⬆︎ to accelerate
  • Hold ⬇︎ to brake
