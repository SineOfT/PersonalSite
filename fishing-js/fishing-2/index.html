Fishing Game 2

This game is a peaceful, quiet game where you are fishing in a vast ocean. There is no fishing meachinisms built into this prototype of the game, so it is mainly an experimentation of how the movement, animations, and visuals play out in a game.

Controls

Keyboard

  • Hold ⬅︎ to move left
  • Hold ⮕ to move right
  • Hold ⬆︎ to move up
  • Hold ⬇︎ to move down
  • Tap Space to tug line
  • Tap z to lock/unlock line
  • Tap Enter to zoom in/out of map

Touch Screen

  • ⬅︎
  • ⮕
  • ⬆︎
  • ⬇︎
  • Space
  • Hold Left of Screen to move left
  • Hold Right of Screen to move right
  • Hold Top of Screen to move up
  • Hold Bottom of Screen to move down
  • Tap Center of Screen to tug line
  • Tap Lock button to lock/unlock line
  • Tap on the map to zoom in/out of map
