Fishing Game 4

The objective of this game is to catch fish in the ocean. However, beware not to put too much strain on your line! If you overstress the line, it will become red and eventually break. Use careful movements, and try to follow the fish's movement in order to keep the catch!

Controls

Keyboard

  • Hold ⬅︎ to move left
  • Hold ⮕ to move right
  • Hold ⬆︎ to move up in ocean
  • Hold ⬇︎ to move down in ocean
  • Tap Space to cast line from surface / tug line in ocean
  • Tap z to lock/unlock line in ocean

Touch Screen

  • ⬅︎
  • ⮕
  • ⬆︎
  • ⬇︎
  • Space
  • Hold Left of Screen to move left
  • Hold Right of Screen to move right
  • Hold Top of Screen to move up in ocean
  • Hold Bottom of Screen to move down in ocean
  • Tap Center of Screen to cast line from surface / tug line in ocean
  • Tap Lock button to lock/unlock line in ocean
