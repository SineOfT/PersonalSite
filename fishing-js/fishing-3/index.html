Fishing Game 3

The objective of this game is to catch fish in the ocean. However, watch out as you need patience and wait for the fish to pull down the line before you can tug the line. The fish can sometimes be fast, so be on guard!

Controls

Keyboard

  • Hold ⬅︎ to move left
  • Hold ⮕ to move right
  • Hold ⬆︎ to move up in ocean
  • Tap Space to cast line from surface / tug line in the ocean
  • Tap z to lock/unlock line in ocean

Touch Screen

  • Hold ◀︎ button to move left
  • Hold ▶︎ button to move right
  • Hold ▲ button to move up in ocean
  • Tap 🎣 button to cast line from surface / tug line in the ocean
  • Tap 🔒 button to lock/unlock line in ocean
